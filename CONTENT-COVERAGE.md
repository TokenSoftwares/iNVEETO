## Product demos (new)

The five chapter demos (address book, invitation list, invitation preview, check-in, campaign) use sample data and carry a visible "Product demo · sample data" label. Every behaviour shown maps to a documented feature (bilingual names, duplicate resolution, VIP/Speaker classification, saved segments, bilingual invitations, custom header/footer, RSVP states, QR check-in, up to 6 check-ins, resend to non-openers, response status).
